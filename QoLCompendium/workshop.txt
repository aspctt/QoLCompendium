description=
description=QoL Compendium takes quality of life mods, rebuilds them for build 42, and merges them into one.
description=
description=They got here different ways. Some were abandoned. Some still work but carry bugs their authors have not got to. Some are freely licensed, and some only lent an idea. All of them are credited below.
description=
description=Most are not ports. Build 41 code rarely survives the move intact, so where the original broke, guessed at a number, or was made obsolete by the base game, it was rewritten against what build 42 actually does.
description=
description=[b]Build 42.20 or newer. Singleplayer and multiplayer. Safe to add to an existing save.[/b]
description=
description=
description=[img]https://i.ibb.co/xtVSd8Kn/Steam-Workshop-Contents-Seperator.png[/img]
description=
description=[b]Rebuilt, and fixed on the way:[/b]
description=
description=[list]
description=    [*][b]XP boosts read as a multiple.[/b] "+75%" is really x4, and Sprinting's first boost is x5. Fitness and Strength stay at x4 however many boosts they get. The original crashes the skills panel on build 42.
description=    [*][b]Generator range.[/b] Build 42 made it a sandbox setting. The original still hardcodes it, so it draws the wrong area and is a floor short upwards for everyone.
description=    [*][b]Flamingos stay visible.[/b] They are drawn in the floor layer, so furniture behind them or snow on them makes them disappear. Still broken in 42.20.
description=    [*][b]Tailoring from cutting clothes.[/b] Vanilla pays nothing for it. Denim and leather now pay per strip, uniforms pay a bonus, and cutting speeds up as the skill rises.
description=    [*][b]Skill book icons.[/b] Build 42 draws ten of the twenty four families with the same tinted sprite. All twenty four have their own now.
description=    [*][b]Container titles.[/b] No longer run into the weight printed beside them.
description=    [*][b]Adrenaline.[/b] Panic sheds fatigue, paid back with interest once you calm down.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Everything else:[/b]
description=
description=[list]
description=    [*][b]Weapon condition[/b] fills the slot with colour as it wears, green through amber to red
description=    [*][b]Generator fuel time[/b], whether or not it is running
description=    [*][b]Screen overlays[/b] for pain, panic, sickness and temperature
description=    [*][b]Fabric type[/b] on clothing tooltips, coloured
description=    [*][b]Flag a book as seen[/b] by reading its first page when it is still too advanced
description=    [*][b]Hotbar reordering[/b], with a lock once the order suits
description=    [*][b]Propane from fuel pumps.[/b] Build 42 has no other way to refill a tank
description=    [*][b]Bigger character avatar[/b] in the info panel
description=    [*][b]Rifle slings[/b], worn front or back
description=    [*][b]Ammo and magazine icons[/b], so five handgun boxes stop sharing one picture
description=    [*][b]Perishable food[/b] split from food that keeps, in the inventory categories
description=    [*][b]A bigger welding torch[/b], and more welding out of a propane tank
description=[/list]
description=
description=Cosmetic settings live in Mod Options, per player. Anything affecting balance is a sandbox option, so a server sets it for everyone instead of each client running its own numbers.
