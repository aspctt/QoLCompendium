description=QoL Compendium takes quality of life mods, rebuilds them for build 42, and merges them into one.
description=
description=They got here different ways. Some were abandoned, some still work but carry bugs, some only lent an idea. All are credited below.
description=
description=Most are not ports. Build 41 code rarely survives the move, so where the original broke or guessed at a number, it was rewritten against what build 42 does.
description=
description=[b]Build 42.20+. Singleplayer and multiplayer. Safe on an existing save.[/b]
description=
description=
description=[img]https://i.ibb.co/xtVSd8Kn/Steam-Workshop-Contents-Seperator.png[/img]

description=[b]Rebuilt, and fixed on the way:[/b]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]XP boosts read as a multiple, not a percentage
description=[*]Generator range follows the sandbox setting
description=[*]Flamingos stay visible behind furniture and under snow
description=[*]Tailoring experience for cutting clothes into strips
description=[*]Shoes, hats, bras, tights, ties, ponchos and empty wallets can be cut into strips at last
description=[*]All twenty four skill book families get their own icon
description=[*]Container titles no longer run into the weight beside them
description=[*]Panic holds tiredness at bay, and pays it back after
description=[*]Houses already standing open no longer sound their alarm
description=[*]Reading a skill book eases boredom, unhappiness and stress
description=[*]Sleep wears off boredom and unhappiness
description=[*]A moodle plate squares off one more quarter per level
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Everything else:[/b]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Weapon condition bars on the hotbar and in your hands
description=[*]Generator fuel time, running or not
description=[*]Screen overlays for pain, panic, sickness and cold
description=[*]Fabric type on clothing tooltips, coloured
description=[*]Flag a book as seen when it is still too advanced
description=[*]Hotbar reordering, with a lock once it suits
description=[*]Propane refills at any working fuel pump
description=[*]Bigger character avatar in the info panel
description=[*]Rifle slings, worn front or back
description=[*]Ammo and magazine icons, one per item
description=[*]Perishable food split from food that keeps
description=[*]A bigger welding torch, and more out of a tank
description=[*]A magazine that teaches the Nutritionist trait
description=[*]Lockpicking, with picks, manuals and a crowbar
description=[*]Sterilized rags usable wherever ripped sheets are
description=[*]Towels and dish cloths tear into rags, and rags sew back into a sheet
description=[*]A torch hangs off your belt, like a screwdriver
description=[/list]
description=
description=Every feature can be turned off on its own, bar one. Cosmetic settings live in Mod Options, per player. Anything affecting balance is a sandbox option, so a server sets it for everyone. The exception is sterilized rag uses, which has to be applied while the game loads its scripts, before any setting exists.
