QoL Compendium takes quality of life mods, rebuilds them for build 42, and merges them into one.

They got here different ways. Some were abandoned. Some still work but carry bugs their authors have not got to. Some are freely licensed, and some only lent an idea. All of them are credited below.

Most are not ports. Build 41 code rarely survives the move intact, so where the original broke, guessed at a number, or was made obsolete by the base game, it was rewritten against what build 42 actually does.

[b]Build 42.20 or newer. Singleplayer and multiplayer. Safe to add to an existing save.[/b]


[img]https://i.ibb.co/xtVSd8Kn/Steam-Workshop-Contents-Seperator.png[/img]

[b]Rebuilt, and fixed on the way:[/b]

[list]
    [*][b]XP boosts read as a multiple.[/b] "+75%" is really x4, and Sprinting's first boost is x5. Fitness and Strength stay at x4 however many boosts they get. The original crashes the skills panel on build 42.
    [*][b]Generator range.[/b] Build 42 made it a sandbox setting. The original still hardcodes it, so it draws the wrong area and is a floor short upwards for everyone.
    [*][b]Flamingos stay visible.[/b] They are drawn in the floor layer, so furniture behind them or snow on them makes them disappear. Still broken in 42.20.
    [*][b]Tailoring from cutting clothes.[/b] Vanilla pays nothing for it. Denim and leather now pay per strip, uniforms pay a bonus, and cutting speeds up as the skill rises.
    [*][b]Skill book icons.[/b] Build 42 draws ten of the twenty four families with the same tinted sprite. All twenty four have their own now.
    [*][b]Container titles.[/b] No longer run into the weight printed beside them.
    [*][b]Adrenaline.[/b] Panic sheds fatigue, paid back with interest once you calm down.
    [*][b]Alarms on open houses.[/b] A house whose door already stands open, or whose window is smashed, no longer sounds its alarm, and neither does a car left unlocked. The original's vehicle half throws on build 42, which moved vehicle parts behind a new accessor.
    [*][b]Reading settles the mind.[/b] A skill book slowly eases boredom, unhappiness and stress. Build 42 gives skill books no morale value at all, only comics and newspapers, and every stat accessor the original used has since been removed.
    [*][b]Sleep on it.[/b] Boredom and unhappiness wear off while you sleep. Build 42 freezes both instead, so a night changes neither.
    [*][b]Moodle quarters.[/b] One more quarter of a moodle's plate squares off for each level, so the stack reads at a glance. Build 42 tints one background by level instead, so the level shapes ship here and the stack is drawn by hand.
[/list]

[b]Everything else:[/b]

[list]
    [*][b]Weapon condition[/b] fills the slot with colour as it wears, green through amber to red
    [*][b]Generator fuel time[/b], whether or not it is running
    [*][b]Screen overlays[/b] for pain, panic, sickness and temperature
    [*][b]Fabric type[/b] on clothing tooltips, coloured
    [*][b]Flag a book as seen[/b] by reading its first page when it is still too advanced
    [*][b]Hotbar reordering[/b], with a lock once the order suits
    [*][b]Propane from fuel pumps.[/b] Build 42 has no other way to refill a tank
    [*][b]Bigger character avatar[/b] in the info panel
    [*][b]Rifle slings[/b], worn front or back
    [*][b]Ammo and magazine icons[/b], so five handgun boxes stop sharing one picture
    [*][b]Perishable food[/b] split from food that keeps, in the inventory categories
    [*][b]A bigger welding torch[/b], and more welding out of a propane tank
[/list]

Every feature can be turned off on its own, so you can take the parts you want and leave the rest.

Cosmetic settings live in Mod Options, per player. Anything affecting balance is a sandbox option, so a server sets it for everyone instead of each client running its own numbers.
